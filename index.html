FAQ

Everything you need to run Emorce.

How do I run Emorce Hub?

Copy the loadstring from the top of this page, paste it into your executor while you are in a supported game, and execute. Enter your key when the gate appears. The loader detects PlaceId and delivers the matching script.

Which executors work?

High-level executors (Wave, Solara, Delta, Volt, Synapse-class, etc.) are supported. Low-level or heavily restricted clients may fail on HttpGet or custom assets.

My game is not listed. Can you add it?

Send the place and script to Axion. New games get wired into the place map and show up here after deploy.

Does the key work outside Roblox?

No. The payload API rejects browsers and non-executor clients. Opening loader.lua in a browser only shows the demorce tamper page.

Why seats / rate limits?

Concurrent seats stop bulk scrapers from dumping every place at once. Legitimate play is fine.
